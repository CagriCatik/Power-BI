# Visualizations — Matrix and Cards

## Overview

This section covers two of the most important structured visuals in Power BI: the **Matrix** and **Cards**. The Matrix is the tabular pivot visual that replaces traditional cross-tab spreadsheet views. Cards display single KPI values prominently and clearly.

Together these two visuals form the backbone of most executive dashboards and summary pages.
